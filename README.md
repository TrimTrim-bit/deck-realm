# Deck Realm - Ultimate Game Platform 🂠

A browser-based arcade with **50 games** across 5 categories — card games, board games, word games, casino games, and classic arcade games. Featuring 44 achievements, 4 card skins, 6 themes, 6 card backs, 6 titles, 3 boosters, 6 pets, a customizable home with furniture, a jukebox with 5 music tracks, and daily login bonuses.

Everything runs offline in your browser. No installation, no server, no internet needed.

## How to Run
1. Double-click `start.bat` on Windows, or open `index.html` in any modern browser.
2. That's it!
